## 1. Scoring Rule

A host that matches the official-domain allowlist is `LOW` with score 0. Otherwise the score sums feature weights: APK 4, login 2, brand keyword 2, and event, free, shortener, and suspicious TLD 1 each. A score of 4 or more is `HIGH`, 2 or 3 is `MEDIUM`, and anything lower is `UNKNOWN`.
